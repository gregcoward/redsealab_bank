RED SEA LAB BANK - FOUNDATION & FIRST BRANCH LOCATION (1956)
================================================================================
Date Founded: October 14, 1956
Co-Founders: Captain Jonathan R. Vance & Dr. Eleanor M. Sterling
Original Name: Red Sea Maritime & Commercial Savings Bank
First Branch Location: 101 Waterfront Way, Suite 100, Seattle, Washington 98101
Initial Charter Purpose: Established in post-war Seattle to provide commercial credit, maritime trade settlement, and secure depository vault services to Pacific Northwest commercial fishing fleets, timber merchants, and maritime trading enterprises.
Historical Context: Founded with an initial capital reserve of $500,000 USD, the inaugural Seattle waterfront office featured a reinforced steel vault and dedicated trade finance desks serving Puget Sound merchant vessels.
