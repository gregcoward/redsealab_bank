RED SEA LAB BANK - KEY HISTORICAL MILESTONES (1956 - PRESENT)
================================================================================
1956 - Bank Foundation: Charter granted in Seattle, WA. Inaugural waterfront branch opens on October 14, 1956.
1974 - California Expansion: Opens San Francisco Financial District flagship at 555 California Street to serve West Coast commercial trade.
1988 - Corporate Rebranding: Rebranded as Red Sea Lab Bank; launches pioneering electronic wire transfer network for corporate accounts.
2005 - Asia-Pacific Gateway: Establishes Honolulu Financial Plaza flagship to process international multi-currency trade between North America and Asia.
2018 - Cloud Infrastructure: Migrates core banking operations to containerized OpenShift microservices and MinIO S3 object storage.
2026 - AI RAG Integration: Deploys 14 Western US Regional Hubs featuring AI-powered assistant interfaces, vector search, and F5 AI Guardrails security.
